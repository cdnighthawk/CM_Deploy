# CODE OF SAFE PRACTICES

**DOCON, INC. dba US Interior Specialties**  
Construction / interiors operations — 8 CCR 1509

These rules apply to every DOCON employee, superintendent, and anyone we supervise. Project packets may add GC rules. The stricter rule wins.

1. Follow these rules. Help others work safely. Report unsafe conditions to the foreman or superintendent at once.
2. Foremen enforce every rule needed for safe work.
3. Accident-prevention instruction is given at least every **10 working days**.
4. No one under the influence of alcohol or a drug that impairs safe work is allowed on the job.
5. No horseplay.
6. Plan the work. Do not leave others exposed to falling objects, open edges, or energized circuits.
7. Do not work when fatigue or illness would expose you or others to injury. Tell the superintendent.
8. Do not enter a confined space until it has been evaluated under the confined-space procedure.
9. Guards stay in place. Report missing guards.
10. Do not crowd onto trucks, lifts, or scaffolds.
11. Do not handle electrical equipment, machinery, or energy sources outside your assignment and training.
12. Report every injury the same shift so treatment can be arranged.
13. Lift with the legs. Get help or a machine for heavy or awkward loads.
14. Wear the PPE listed on the Project Safety Packet. No worn-out soles. Hard hat, glasses, and work boots are the default on active sites.
15. Do not throw material from a structure until the area below is barricaded and people are warned.
16. Use GFCI protection on temporary 120-volt construction power. Take damaged cords out of service.
17. Ladders: face the ladder, three points of contact, do not stand on the top two steps of a stepladder, extend extension ladders 3 feet above the landing, do not use metal ladders near energized parts.
18. Stop work and warn others if you disturb suspected asbestos or lead. Only a licensed contractor abates it.
19. You may stop work that is an imminent hazard. You will not be punished for a good-faith stop or report.

Acknowledgement of this Code is part of new-hire orientation and project orientation.
