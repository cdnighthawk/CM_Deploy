FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this Procore?

No. The public page follows the same kind of construction-SaaS homepage — nav, hero, audiences, platform, AI, field, FAQ — because that is how buyers scan a category. The product is WorX CM for the trade that owns the package. We do not clone Procore modules, orange chrome, or guest-seat economics.

What is the difference between WorX CM, USIS, and FinishWorks Field?

WorX CM is the public product name (worxcm.com). US Interior Specialties (USIS) is the legal company. Staff login remains on usiscm.com until cutover. FinishWorks Field is the store name of the native phone apps only.

Do you email plan sets to vendors?

No. Vendors get a tokenized files page. Bytes live in a private snapshot and download on a short-lived authorized URL. From / Reply-To / BCC stay on quotes@gousis.com.

Does Local AI send mail or file government forms?

No. AI never emails vendors, never dumps correspondence to Grok, never files EDD, never pushes punch items to Procore on its own, and never writes an SSN onto a User record.
